Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

PDFReady provides free browser-based PDF and image tools. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use this website.

1. Files You Upload

PDFReady's tools are designed to process supported files directly in your web browser. Files selected for processing are not intentionally uploaded to a PDFReady server by the website's basic tools.

2. Personal Information

PDFReady does not require users to create an account to use the basic tools. We do not intentionally collect names, phone numbers, addresses or other personal information through the basic tools.

3. Cookies and Advertising

PDFReady may use third-party services such as analytics or advertising services in the future. Such services may use cookies or similar technologies according to their own privacy policies.

4. Third-Party Services

Some PDF processing libraries are loaded from third-party content delivery networks. Those services may process technical information such as browser requests according to their own policies.

5. Security

We take reasonable steps to provide a safe website. However, no website or internet transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

6. Children's Privacy

PDFReady is not specifically directed toward children and does not knowingly request personal information from children.

7. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the website's features or services change. The updated version will be published on this page.

8. Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy, use the contact method provided on the PDFReady website.
